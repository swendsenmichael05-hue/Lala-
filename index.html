How to play

One player chooses an action; the other helps or prepares an escape. Both roll a d20. A matching knack adds 2. A helper total of 10 adds 3 to the action, or protects your luck if you chose an escape.

Reach the target to succeed. A natural 1 fails; a natural 20 succeeds. Failure costs 1 shared luck, or 2 on a natural 1. A natural 20 restores 1 luck. Finish eight encounters before your luck runs out.

Each new trip assembles a new world and cast from authored rules, conflicts and situations. Names and world configurations are checked against saved history. The situation structures are finite, not infinitely authored stories. Clearing browser storage clears repeat protection.
