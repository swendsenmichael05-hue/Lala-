One little trip. Two people.

Choose. One person picks what to do. The other decides how to help. You swap jobs at the next stop.

Roll. These are ordinary six-sided dice. Your partner rolls first. On 3 or higher, they either make your roll easier or keep you safe if it fails.

See what happens. Meet the number shown and your plan works. A failed plan costs one shared chance unless your partner protects you. No extra stats to remember.

There are eight stops and five chances. At zero, you find a safe way home. Every new trip gets a new place and cast. Story ingredients can recur; saved history helps keep recent situations apart.
